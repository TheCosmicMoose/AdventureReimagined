**Moose Flavored**

This pack aims to take some of the tweaks made by Klei Wright's Matcha Flavored pack, while keeping the majority of the game vanilla. Some of the key things I wanted to include are easier access to shulker boxes (I feel like I always end up going to the end before I really want to just so I can have shulkers). I also love Matcha's reward for killing the dragon being a safe overworld surface. I think that this is a more meaningful reward for "Beating the Game" than what vanilla minecraft offers. However I am executing it differently so that mobs still spawn above sea level, keeping mob farms intact.

My goal is to "Matchafy" vanilla without completely changing the game's overall progression and mechanics, and keeping farms intact.

**Key changes that you need to know about before playing:**
- In addition to killing cows for leather, you can also use rotten flesh to make leather using a campfire or smoker.
- Shulker boxes can be crafted using leather, string, and either a barrel or a chest. Once you pick up leather, you should have this recipe unlocked, but if it doesn't appear, you can craft it as follows:
LSL
LCL
LLL
With L= leather, S= string, and C= chest/barrel.
- Ender chests are crafted using ender pearls, a chest, and obsidian. Crafted with obsidian around the sides and bottom, chest in the center, and pearl in the top center.
- Eyes of ender are crafted using 4 ender pearls, a blaze rod, a breeze rod, an echo shard, a prismarine shard and a totem of undying. This recipe should be unlocked when you pick up either an ender pearl or a blaze rod, but if it does not appear, you can craft it as follows:
Pearl, Blaze Rod, Pearl
Prismarine, Totem, Echo
Pearl, Breeze Rod, Pearl
This recipe produces 16 Eyes of Ender, which should be plenty to get you to the stronghold and fill the portal.

This means to access the end you need to visit: a nether fortress, an ocean monument, an ancient city, a trial chamber, and either summon a raid or visit a woodland mansion. My goal in this is to require players to engage with more of their world before going to the end, while keeping it as vanilla-friendly as possible.

Killing the Ender Dragon now gives the substantial reward of eliminating most mob spawns from the surface of the overworld. Specifically, mobs cannot spawn under the open sky. So you still need to light up your base, and take care going through forests or near caves, because if a mob spawns with a solid block overhead, it will still be able to spawn, and once spawned it can move freely. But in general the overworld should be much more peaceful.
